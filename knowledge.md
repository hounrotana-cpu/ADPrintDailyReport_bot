# ADPrint — Starter knowledge (owner must review before use)
រោងពុម្ព ADPrint — printing and packaging.
Products: ប្រអប់, ថង់ក្រដាស, ស្ទីកគ័រ, សៀវភៅ, Catalog, Folder, Flyer/Brochure, Business Card, A1 printing.
Quotation information to collect: product, finished dimensions and units, quantity, material and gsm, print colors and sides, finishing, artwork readiness, requested delivery date and delivery location.
For books also ask page count, inside paper, cover paper, binding and number of copies.
For stickers ask dimensions, shape, quantity, surface/use conditions, paper or PVC and lamination requirement.
Suggested workflow: Sales → Design/Prepress → Production planning → Printing → required finishing → QC → Delivery. Actual stages depend on the product.
Before production: confirm artwork, size, quantity, material, finishing and approved quotation with responsible staff.
Prices, promotions, deposit percentages, lead times, stock availability and discounts are NOT configured. Refer to Sales Supervisor for approval. Do not use old promotional prices.
Customer artwork, product photos and documents must not be posted or shared externally without approval.
This bot only gives advice and drafts. It does not create real quotations, invoices, ERP records or production jobs.
